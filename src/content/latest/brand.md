## Aimii — A Creative Label

Aimii is more than a logo.  
It’s a label.

A creative umbrella that brings together the three things I care about most:

- **Design & Technology**
- **Art & Visual Expression**
- **Music & Sound**

Aimii represents the intersection of structured systems and emotional output — logic and intuition coexisting.

### 🎨 A Flexible Identity

The Aimii mark was designed to live across contexts.

It can:

- Shift colours
- Adapt backgrounds
- Invert light and dark
- Exist as icon-only or full wordmark
- Sit comfortably in tech environments or creative spaces

No matter the variation, it retains clarity and personality.

It’s playful but intentional.  
Soft but confident.  
Modern but human.

The goal was to create something modular — a visual system that feels consistent without being rigid.

### 🧠 What Aimii Means To Me

Aimii is my container.

It’s where:

- Code becomes product
- Interfaces become experiences
- Sound becomes atmosphere
- Ideas become something tangible

It represents independence, experimentation, and long-term thinking.

I’ve spent years building platforms for broadcasters, streaming services, and live systems.  
Aimii is where I build for myself — and for collaborators who value craft.

### 🚀 What I Hope It Becomes

I see Aimii evolving into:

- A creative studio
- A digital label
- A space for artists and technologists
- A home for music releases
- A platform for experimental work
- A place where systems and art meet

Not just a portfolio.  
Not just a brand.  
But a foundation for building things that feel alive.

Aimii is structured like my code:

Modular.  
Scalable.  
Adaptable.  

But it carries the emotional weight of my art and music.

That duality is intentional.
